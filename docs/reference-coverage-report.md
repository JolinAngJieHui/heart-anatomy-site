# Cardiovascular source coverage review

Checked 11 October 2026. 23 unique supplied references are registered. PMC12060931 appeared twice and was deduplicated. All new records are pending_review; none is approved or eligible for production retrieval. Website teaching text and synthetic test fixtures remain unchanged.

Coverage means a candidate supports a topic, not that it has passed academic or rights review. Direct and parent-vessel mappings count toward candidate normal-anatomy levels; clinical context, unverified locators and rare cases do not. Levels indicate proposed educational use, not publisher-provided grading. Verify precise passages during academic review.

## Numbering and variants

The website's 1–17 are teaching annotations, comprising arteries, a great artery and veins. They are neither the SCCT 18 coronary segments nor the AHA 17 myocardial segments. Left Coronary Artery means left main; apical LAD needs an explicit reviewer mapping to any distal-LAD convention. Generic OM is not automatically OM1 or OM2. Never use annotation numbers as segmentation codes. The [Villa review, Figure 3](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4919754/) and [ASCI-PT consensus](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10973734/) describe separate schemes.

Rare cadaveric LCx/right-sinus/LCA cases are flagged rare-anomaly and Advanced only. They provide variant context, not standard origins. Separate conus ostia and dominance variation are not automatically rare anomalies. Prevalence estimates and source conflicts need reviewer reconciliation. The Cleveland Clinic LAD dominance passage is explicitly flagged for cross-checking.
