## right-marginal-artery-1: physiology

Pattern: normal; relationship: direct; structures: right-marginal-artery; levels: basic, intermediate, advanced.

The right marginal, or acute marginal, branch leaves the RCA near the acute margin, follows the border toward the apex and supplies the right ventricular free wall.

Evidence: [pmc4919754: Coronary artery anomalies overview: The normal and the abnormal](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4919754/) — NORMAL ANATOMY — RCA. World Journal of Radiology 8(6):537–555; 2016-06-28. Registry source version: 1. Access: primary-search-excerpt.
