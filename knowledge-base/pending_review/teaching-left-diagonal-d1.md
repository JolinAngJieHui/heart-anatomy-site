## left-diagonal-d1-1: physiology

Pattern: normal; relationship: direct; structures: left-diagonal-d1; levels: basic, intermediate, advanced.

D1 is a named LAD diagonal branch. Diagonal branches travel laterally and supply the anterolateral left ventricular wall; their number varies.

Evidence: [radiologyassistant-coronary: Coronary anatomy and anomalies](https://radiologyassistant.nl/cardiovascular/anatomy/coronary-anatomy-and-anomalies) — Left Anterior Descending (LAD).
