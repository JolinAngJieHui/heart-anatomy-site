## left-anterior-descending-proximal-1: anatomy

Pattern: normal; relationship: direct; structures: left-anterior-descending-proximal; levels: intermediate, advanced.

ASCI describes SCCT proximal LAD from the end of left main to the first large septal branch or significant D1, whichever is closer to the origin; the cited D1 criterion exceeds 1.5 mm.

Evidence: [pmc10973734: 2024 Consensus Statement on Coronary Stenosis and Plaque Evaluation in CT Angiography From the Asian Society of Cardiovascular Imaging-Practical Tutorial (ASCI-PT)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10973734/) — Issue 2 — Proximal vs. mid-LAD. Korean Journal of Radiology 25(4):331–342; 2024-03-20; April issue. Registry source version: 1. Access: readable-page.

## left-anterior-descending-proximal-2: physiology

Pattern: normal; relationship: direct; structures: left-anterior-descending-proximal; levels: basic, intermediate, advanced.

Through septal and diagonal branches, the parent LAD supplies anterior septal and left ventricular myocardium. This is parent-vessel context, not a fixed proximal-segment territory.

Evidence: [cc-lad: Left Anterior Descending Artery](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/body/23985-left-anterior-descending-artery) — Anatomy — What are the branches of the left anterior descending artery?. Cleveland Clinic Health Library; medically reviewed; updated 2022-08-04. Registry source version: 1. Access: readable-page.
