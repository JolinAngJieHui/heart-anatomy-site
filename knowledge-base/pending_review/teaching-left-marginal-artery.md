## left-marginal-artery-1: physiology

Pattern: normal; relationship: direct; structures: left-marginal-artery; levels: basic, intermediate, advanced.

The left marginal label here denotes the obtuse marginal family of LCx branches supplying lateral left ventricular myocardium. It is not automatically a specific OM1 or OM2.

Evidence: [radiologyassistant-coronary: Coronary anatomy and anomalies](https://radiologyassistant.nl/cardiovascular/anatomy/coronary-anatomy-and-anomalies) — Circumflex (Cx). The Radiology Assistant; Rijnland Hospital Leiderdorp and University Medical Centre Groningen; published 2008-10-14. Registry source version: 1. Access: readable-page.

## left-marginal-artery-2: anatomy

Pattern: normal-variant; relationship: direct; structures: left-marginal-artery; levels: advanced.

ASCI distinguishes a ramus intermedius arising at a left-main trifurcation from an early LCx-derived OM1. Parent-vessel origin matters more than proximity alone.

Evidence: [pmc10973734: 2024 Consensus Statement on Coronary Stenosis and Plaque Evaluation in CT Angiography From the Asian Society of Cardiovascular Imaging-Practical Tutorial (ASCI-PT)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10973734/) — Issue 3 — Ramus intermedius vs. first obtuse marginal artery. Korean Journal of Radiology 25(4):331–342; 2024-03-20; April issue. Registry source version: 1. Access: readable-page.
