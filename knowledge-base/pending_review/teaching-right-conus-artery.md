## right-conus-artery-1: physiology

Pattern: normal-variant; relationship: direct; structures: right-conus-artery; levels: basic, intermediate, advanced.

The conus branch supplies the right ventricular outflow tract. It often branches from RCA; a separate aortic origin is an anatomical variant rather than the typical RCA-branch pattern.

Evidence: [radiologyassistant-coronary: Coronary anatomy and anomalies](https://radiologyassistant.nl/cardiovascular/anatomy/coronary-anatomy-and-anomalies) — Right Coronary Artery (RCA).
