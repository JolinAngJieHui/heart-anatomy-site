## right-coronary-proximal-1: anatomy

Pattern: normal; relationship: direct; structures: right-coronary-proximal; levels: basic, intermediate, advanced.

The proximal RCA starts at the right aortic sinus and follows the right atrioventricular groove. In Villa's scheme it ends halfway between the origin and acute margin.

Evidence: [pmc4919754: Coronary artery anomalies overview: The normal and the abnormal](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4919754/) — NORMAL ANATOMY — RCA. World Journal of Radiology 8(6):537–555; 2016-06-28. Registry source version: 1. Access: primary-search-excerpt.

## right-coronary-proximal-2: physiology

Pattern: normal; relationship: direct; structures: right-coronary-proximal; levels: basic, intermediate, advanced.

The RCA supplies the right atrium and right ventricle. Nodal supply through its branches varies; a fixed territory must not be assigned to every RCA segment.

Evidence: [cc-coronary-arteries: Coronary Arteries](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/body/22973-coronary-arteries) — Function — What is the purpose of the coronary arteries?. Cleveland Clinic Health Library; medically reviewed; updated 2025-06-02. Registry source version: 1. Access: readable-page.

## right-coronary-proximal-3: clinical

Pattern: clinical-context; relationship: general-coronary-context; structures: right-coronary-proximal, right-coronary-middle, right-coronary-distal, posterior-descending-artery, left-coronary-artery, left-anterior-descending-proximal, left-anterior-descending-mid, left-anterior-descending-apical, left-diagonal-d1, left-diagonal-d2, left-circumflex-artery, right-marginal-artery, right-conus-artery, left-marginal-artery; levels: basic, intermediate, advanced.

Coronary atherosclerosis can narrow arteries and reduce myocardial oxygen delivery, causing ischemia or infarction. Angiography, PCI and bypass surgery are general diagnostic/procedural contexts; they do not establish a branch-specific indication.

Evidence: [cc-cad: Coronary Artery Disease](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/diseases/16898-coronary-artery-disease) — Overview; Diagnosis and Tests; Management and Treatment.
