## right-coronary-distal-1: anatomy

Pattern: normal; relationship: direct; structures: right-coronary-distal; levels: basic, intermediate, advanced.

In Villa's scheme the distal RCA continues from the acute margin toward the base, at the atrial/ventricular septal junction. This distinguishes it from the origin-to-halfway proximal segment.

Evidence: [pmc4919754: Coronary artery anomalies overview: The normal and the abnormal](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4919754/) — NORMAL ANATOMY — RCA. World Journal of Radiology 8(6):537–555; 2016-06-28. Registry source version: 1. Access: primary-search-excerpt.
