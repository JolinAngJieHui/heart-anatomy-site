## left-coronary-artery-1: anatomy

Pattern: normal; relationship: direct; structures: left-coronary-artery; levels: basic, intermediate, advanced.

The left main coronary artery arises at the left aortic sinus, passes between the right ventricular outflow tract and left atrium, and divides into LAD and LCx.

Evidence: [radiologyassistant-coronary: Coronary anatomy and anomalies](https://radiologyassistant.nl/cardiovascular/anatomy/coronary-anatomy-and-anomalies) — Left Coronary Artery (LCA). The Radiology Assistant; Rijnland Hospital Leiderdorp and University Medical Centre Groningen; published 2008-10-14. Registry source version: 1. Access: readable-page.

## left-coronary-artery-2: anatomy

Pattern: normal; relationship: direct; structures: left-coronary-artery; levels: intermediate, advanced.

For the SCCT classification discussed by ASCI, the left main extends from its ostium to the LAD/LCx bifurcation. This is a reporting definition.

Evidence: [pmc10973734: 2024 Consensus Statement on Coronary Stenosis and Plaque Evaluation in CT Angiography From the Asian Society of Cardiovascular Imaging-Practical Tutorial (ASCI-PT)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10973734/) — Issue 1 — Distal margin of the left main artery. Korean Journal of Radiology 25(4):331–342; 2024-03-20; April issue. Registry source version: 1. Access: readable-page.

## left-coronary-artery-3: physiology

Pattern: normal; relationship: general-coronary-context; structures: right-coronary-proximal, right-coronary-middle, right-coronary-distal, posterior-descending-artery, left-coronary-artery, left-anterior-descending-proximal, left-anterior-descending-mid, left-anterior-descending-apical, left-diagonal-d1, left-diagonal-d2, left-circumflex-artery, right-marginal-artery, right-conus-artery, left-marginal-artery; levels: intermediate, advanced.

Coronary perfusion delivers oxygen to active myocardium. Ventricular contraction compresses intramyocardial vessels; relaxation permits increased flow. Reduced diastolic time can impair perfusion, particularly in the left ventricle.

Evidence: [nbk482413: Physiology, Coronary Circulation](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK482413/) — Mechanism — Blood flow; Regulation. StatPearls [Internet], StatPearls Publishing; chapter updated 2023-05-01; current container 2026 Jan. Registry source version: 1. Access: primary-search-excerpt.
