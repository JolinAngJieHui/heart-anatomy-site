# Attributed review summary — pending academic approval

Based on Kesieme, Omoregbee, Ngaage and Danton, *Comprehensive Review of Coronary Artery Anatomy Relevant to Cardiac Surgery* (2025 issue; online 2024), CC BY 4.0. Original: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12060931/ . This is a shortened original paraphrase, not the full article; no illustrations reproduced.

The RCA originates at the right aortic sinus and follows the right atrioventricular groove. The left main originates at the left sinus and divides into LAD and LCx. The LAD follows the anterior interventricular course toward the apex. The LCx follows the left atrioventricular groove and gives obtuse marginal branches. PDA origin varies with coronary dominance; a right-coronary origin is not universal. These vessel descriptions do not define the website's segment boundaries.
