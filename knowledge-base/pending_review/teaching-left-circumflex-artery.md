## left-circumflex-artery-1: anatomy

Pattern: normal; relationship: direct; structures: left-circumflex-artery; levels: basic, intermediate, advanced.

The LCx normally branches from left main and travels in the groove between left atrium and ventricle. It gives off variably numbered obtuse marginal branches.

## left-circumflex-artery-2: physiology

Pattern: normal; relationship: direct; structures: left-circumflex-artery; levels: basic, intermediate, advanced.

The LCx supplies left-sided myocardial regions through its branches. Its distribution, including nodal contribution, varies between individuals.

Evidence: [cc-circumflex: Circumflex Artery](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/body/23926-circumflex-artery) — Function — What does the circumflex artery do?. Cleveland Clinic Health Library; medically reviewed; updated 2022-07-28. Registry source version: 1. Access: readable-page.

## left-circumflex-artery-3: clinical

Pattern: clinical-context; relationship: direct; structures: left-circumflex-artery; levels: intermediate, advanced.

LCx atherosclerotic narrowing may involve a branch-point lesion; angioplasty and stenting are procedural context, not treatment advice or a universal indication.

Evidence: [cc-circumflex: Circumflex Artery](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/body/23926-circumflex-artery) — Overview; Conditions and Disorders. Cleveland Clinic Health Library; medically reviewed; updated 2022-07-28. Registry source version: 1. Access: readable-page.

## left-circumflex-artery-4: anatomy

Pattern: rare-anomaly; relationship: direct; structures: left-circumflex-artery; levels: advanced.

A cadaveric report described a retroaortic LCx arising with RCA/conus ostia at the right aortic sinus. This is not typical anatomy and does not redefine the usual left-main origin.

Evidence: [pmc10749686: Anomalous Left Circumflex Artery Arising From the Right Coronary Artery: A Cadaveric Case Report and Review of the Literature](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10749686/) — Abstract (also verified through PubMed 38146550). Cureus 15(11):e49380; 2023-11-25. Registry source version: 1. Access: readable-page.
