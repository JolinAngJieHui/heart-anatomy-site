## ascending-aorta-1: anatomy

Pattern: normal; relationship: direct; structures: ascending-aorta; levels: basic, intermediate, advanced.

The ascending aorta is the upward thoracic portion leading from the heart toward the arch. Coronary origins are associated with the root region; root and ascending-aorta labels need explicit boundaries.

## ascending-aorta-2: physiology

Pattern: normal; relationship: direct; structures: ascending-aorta; levels: basic, intermediate, advanced.

The ascending aorta conducts oxygenated blood leaving the left ventricle onward into the systemic arterial circulation.

Evidence: [cc-aorta: Aorta](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/body/17058-aorta-anatomy) — Function — What is the function of the aorta?. Cleveland Clinic Health Library; medically reviewed; updated 2022-02-24. Registry source version: 1. Access: readable-page.

## ascending-aorta-3: clinical

Pattern: clinical-context; relationship: direct; structures: ascending-aorta; levels: intermediate, advanced.

Root dilatation, thoracic aneurysm and dissection are relevant aortic disease contexts. A dissection involves separation within the wall after an inner-lining tear; precise procedural thresholds require specialist evidence.
