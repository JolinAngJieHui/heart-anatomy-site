## left-anterior-descending-apical-1: anatomy

Pattern: normal; relationship: direct; structures: left-anterior-descending-apical; levels: basic, intermediate, advanced.

The LAD runs toward the apex along the anterior interventricular region. The website's apical LAD label denotes the apex region and requires a reviewed crosswalk to formal distal-segment definitions.

Evidence: [cc-lad: Left Anterior Descending Artery](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/body/23985-left-anterior-descending-artery) — Anatomy — Where is the left anterior descending artery located?. ## left-anterior-descending-apical-2: physiology

Pattern: normal-variant; relationship: direct; structures: left-anterior-descending-apical; levels: intermediate, advanced.

A wraparound LAD may supply apical inferior myocardium. This is a normal variant and must not be assumed in every heart.

Evidence: [pmc4919754: Coronary artery anomalies overview: The normal and the abnormal](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4919754/) — NORMAL VARIANTS — Inferior wall supply. World Journal of Radiology 8(6):537–555; 2016-06-28. Registry source version: 1. Access: primary-search-excerpt.
