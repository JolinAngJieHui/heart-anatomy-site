## posterior-descending-artery-1: physiology

Pattern: normal; relationship: direct; structures: posterior-descending-artery; levels: basic, intermediate, advanced.

The PDA, or posterior interventricular artery, follows the posterior interventricular groove and supplies inferior myocardium and the inferior septum.

Evidence: [radiologyassistant-coronary: Coronary anatomy and anomalies](https://radiologyassistant.nl/cardiovascular/anatomy/coronary-anatomy-and-anomalies) — Right Coronary Artery (RCA). The Radiology Assistant; Rijnland Hospital Leiderdorp and University Medical Centre Groningen; published 2008-10-14. Registry source version: 1. Access: readable-page.

## posterior-descending-artery-2: anatomy

Pattern: normal-variant; relationship: direct; structures: posterior-descending-artery; levels: intermediate, advanced.

Coronary dominance concerns PDA and posterolateral origins: RCA in right dominance, LCx in left dominance, and contributions from both systems in codominance. These are normal variations.

Evidence: [pmc4919754: Coronary artery anomalies overview: The normal and the abnormal](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4919754/) — NORMAL VARIANTS — Coronary dominance. World Journal of Radiology 8(6):537–555; 2016-06-28. Registry source version: 1. Access: primary-search-excerpt.
